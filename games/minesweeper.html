How to play

  • Click a square to uncover it. A number tells you how many of the 8 squares around it hide a mine.
  • Right-click (or turn on Flag mode) to mark a square you think is a mine.
  • Click an uncovered number whose mines are all flagged to open everything around it.
  • Keyboard: move with the arrow keys, Enter or Space to uncover, F to flag.
  • Your first click is always safe.
