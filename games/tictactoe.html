How to play

  • Take turns placing X and O. Three in a row — across, down, or diagonal — wins.
  • You are X against the computer. X and O swap who goes first each game.
  • Keyboard: Tab to a square, or press 1–9 (laid out like a phone keypad, top-left is 1).
