BlockCraft

Mine, build, craft, and survive the night, then summon and defeat the Sky Dragon. Runs entirely in this one page; nothing to install.

Controls
  • Click the game to capture the mouse, then move the mouse to look. Esc pauses.
  • WASD move, Space jump/swim, Shift sprint.
  • Hold left click to mine or attack. Right click to place a block, eat food, or use a crafting table/furnace.
  • 1–9 or the mouse wheel pick a hotbar slot. E opens your inventory and crafting.
  • Creative: double-tap Space or press F to fly; Space/Shift go up/down.
  • T chats in multiplayer. F3 shows coordinates.
How to beat the game
  1. Punch a tree for logs, then craft planks, sticks and a crafting table.
  2. Make a wooden pickaxe, mine stone, and upgrade to stone tools. Craft a furnace.
  3. Find coal and iron ore underground. Smelt iron in the furnace and make iron tools.
  4. Dig deep (below height 14) for diamonds.
  5. Craft a Dragon Altar (3 diamonds, 4 iron, 2 cobblestone) and place it to summon the Sky Dragon.
  6. Hit the dragon when it swoops at you or lands by the altar. Tip: a Mace hits much harder if you jump down onto your target.

Invite a friend

GitHub Pages can only host files; it can't run a game server. So your computers connect straight to each other after you swap two codes once (send them by chat, email, or anything else).

  1. Click Make invite code and send the code to your friend.
  2. Your friend opens BlockCraft, clicks Join a friend, pastes it, and sends you back their reply code.
  3. Paste the reply code below and click Connect. Repeat for each extra friend.

Join a friend
