BlockCraft

Explore an endless world: mine, build, craft, survive the night, travel through the Nether, find a stronghold, and defeat the Ender Dragon in the End. Runs entirely in this one page; nothing to install.

Controls
  • Click the game to capture the mouse, then move the mouse to look. Esc pauses.
  • WASD move, Space jump/swim, Shift sprint.
  • Hold left click to mine or attack. Right click to place a block, eat food, or use a crafting table/furnace.
  • 1–9 or the mouse wheel pick a hotbar slot. E opens your inventory and crafting.
  • Creative: double-tap Space or press F to fly; Space/Shift go up/down.
  • T chats in multiplayer. F3 shows coordinates.
How to beat the game
  1. Punch a tree for logs. Press E and craft planks, sticks and a crafting table in your 2×2 grid. The recipe book shows every pattern and can fill the grid for you.
  2. Right-click the crafting table for the 3×3 grid. Make a wooden pickaxe, mine stone, and upgrade to stone tools. Craft a furnace.
  3. Find coal and iron ore underground. Right-click the furnace, put iron ore on top and coal below, and make iron tools.
  4. Dig deep (below height 14) for diamonds.
  5. Make a diamond pickaxe and mine obsidian (found deep down, near the bottom of the world). Break gravel for flint and craft flint and steel.
  6. Build an obsidian frame 4 wide and 5 tall (a 2×3 hole) and light it to open a portal to the Nether.
  7. In the Nether, find a nether-brick fortress and fight blazes for blaze rods. Craft them into blaze powder.
  8. Back in the Overworld, hunt endermen at night for ender pearls (don't look at their faces!). Pearl + blaze powder = Eye of Ender.
  9. Throw eyes (right-click) and follow them to a stronghold underground. Put eyes in all 12 frames of its portal and jump in.
  10. In the End, break the crystals on the pillars (they heal the dragon), then defeat the Ender Dragon. Tip: a Mace hits much harder if you jump down onto your target.
