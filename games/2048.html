How to play

  • Slide all tiles with the arrow keys or W A S D, the buttons, or by swiping.
  • Two tiles with the same number merge into one. Reach the 2048 tile to win, then keep going if you like.
  • Made a mistake? U or the Undo button takes back one move. Your game is saved if you leave the page.
